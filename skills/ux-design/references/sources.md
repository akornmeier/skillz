## Citation practice

- Link the specific law page when naming a principle in a deliverable.
- For high-stakes or disputed claims, follow the page’s further-reading trail and cite the primary study or an authoritative synthesis.
- Record the exact version, access date, population, task, and limits of any empirical evidence used.
- Do not turn a memorable threshold or ratio into a universal requirement without contextual evidence.
